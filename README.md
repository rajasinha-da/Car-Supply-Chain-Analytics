# Car Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard – Power BI
## Project Overview

    This project focuses on analyzing car supply chain operations using Power BI. The objective was to transform raw supply chain data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards. The report helps stakeholders monitor sales performance, inventory movement, supplier efficiency, logistics performance, and overall supply chain trends.

## Business Problem

The organization needed better visibility into its supply chain operations to answer key business questions:

How are sales and revenue performing across different regions and vehicle categories?
Which suppliers and manufacturers are contributing the most value?
What are the major factors affecting delivery performance?
How efficiently is inventory being managed?
Which areas require operational improvement?

The goal was to create a centralized analytics solution that enables faster and data-driven decision-making.

## Dataset Description

### The dataset contains car supply chain information including:
    Vehicle details
    Supplier and manufacturer information
    Order information
    Sales transactions
    Inventory levels
    Logistics and delivery details
    Location and regional data
    Cost and revenue-related fields

The dataset was cleaned, transformed, and modeled to create an efficient analytical data model.
